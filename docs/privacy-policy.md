# Xiangqi privacy policy

Updated 4 October 2026

Xiangqi is developed by Frank Zhu. The app works offline and does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer. It has no accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics, or automatic diagnostic uploads. It requests no runtime permissions and has no network access.

## Data on your device

Saved games, learning progress, and settings stay in the app’s storage on your device. The bundled computer engine processes positions locally. Automatic Android backup of app data is turned off; your device may still include app data in a device-to-device transfer according to your device settings.

## Sharing and external links

When you choose Share, the game record is passed to the recipient or service you select. Opening source-code or support links takes you to GitHub, which applies its own privacy policy. Information you submit in a GitHub issue may be public; avoid including private information.

## Retention and deletion

Local data remains until it is replaced or you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android Settings. Copies you have shared must be managed separately with their respective services. The developer holds no server copy of your games or settings.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact Frank Zhu through the linked GitHub issue tracker. Google may process Play Store, device-backup, and crash information under its own privacy settings and policies.

Support: [Xiangqi Mobile for Android issue tracker](https://github.com/frankmanzhu/xiangqi-mobile-android/issues).

External services: [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) and [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
